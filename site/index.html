How values are calculated
  • z-score per category = (per-game stat − pool mean) ÷ pool SD. FG% and FT% use impact = attempts × (player% − pool%). TO is negated.
  • Value = sum of category z-scores, per game. Total = value × games played in the window.
  • The pool approximates rostered players: the top teams × roster spots by value, found by iterating until stable. Only players with enough games count toward it.
  • The last-7-days window uses the season-to-date mean and SD, so its z-scores read as "playing at this level lately".
  • A dot marks players in the season-to-date pool. Players below the minimum games don't count toward it, so the pool can leave out stars who have missed time.
  • "Hide likely rostered" hides pool players and anyone in the top N by per-game value this season or last season. What's left are the likelier waiver candidates. It's an estimate, not your league's actual rosters.
